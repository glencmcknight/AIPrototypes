Design Log — Prototype 01

Snake: still full of opportunity

Snake is one of my all-time favorite games, and even decades later it still feels like there's real room to expand and explore within its simple ruleset. That made it a good first project for trying an AI-assisted build — though honestly, the process felt a lot less like "vibe coding" and a lot more like how I already build: small, testable chunks, tunable variables, and a lot of playing what I've made before deciding what comes next.

Get it functional, get it familiar

The very first step wasn't design — it was structure. Before adding a single new idea, I wanted the underlying code to follow patterns I already think in: a set of tunable variables up top, a clear construction step, and an update loop I could reason about. The AI was supportive of building it that way from the start, which made everything after this step easier to trust.

The game itself just needed to play in the classic way — no twists yet. Get the loop right before touching the design.

Obstacles: interrupt the default loop

Standard Snake has one lever: it gets faster until your reaction time can't keep up. That's a real addictive loop, but it's also the only thing happening. Obstacles were the first real design move — fixed shapes on the board that force you to navigate and plan a route, rather than just react faster.

A world that grows

The next thing I wanted to chase was a feeling I find endlessly satisfying as a player: a world that grows and opens up. I'm an explorer by nature, so a board that expands as you succeed felt like the right next layer — more space, more obstacles, more room to keep making decisions.

04
Where I'd take it next

Multi-screen, Sokoban-influenced exploration

The idea I'd like to chase further is a multi-screen world — traveling from location to location, each one ever-growing, with some problems borrowed from Sokoban-style block-pushing rather than pure reflex play. That would turn Snake from a single escalating loop into something closer to a small world to explore and solve.
